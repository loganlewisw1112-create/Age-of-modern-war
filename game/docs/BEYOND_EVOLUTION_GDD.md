# BEYOND EVOLUTION — DLC III (G21)
Owner-directed planning, 2026-11-27. Planned, not implemented. Requires accepted G18, G19 and G20; no changes to frozen gates. Offline solo persistent civilization experience; optional compatible owned content in Skirmish subject to existing entitlement and multiplayer rules. No mandatory online services.
## Player promise
Continue the inherited universe, commanders, colonies, relationships and history. Begin with individual planetary terraforming and foundational Dyson projects; progressively accelerate and automate earlier work as genuinely new capabilities unlock. Create worlds, life and civilizations, defend them against Vhessar or other autonomous powers. Classic RTS selection, construction, combat and delegation remain central; advanced editors optional. Existing mode-specific direct-control restrictions remain intact.
## Six capability eras
1. Planetary transformation: atmosphere, climate, water, soil, radiation, vegetation, habitability, regional restoration, first Dyson collectors and engineering colonies.
2. Stellar engineering: swarms/spheres, orbital manufacturing, energy networks, concurrent terraforming, engineering fleets and defenses.
3. System architecture: artificial planets/moons, ringworlds, hollow, ocean, fortress, ecological and machine worlds, orbital cities and mobile habitats.
4. Biological engineering: fictional genetic traits, splicing, hybridization, synthetic/organic-machine life, extinct species restoration, ecosystem seeding.
5. Civilization creation: intelligent species, independent settlements, languages, economies, doctrines, diplomacy and expansion.
6. Beyond Evolution: stellar stabilization, fictional stellar material extraction, orbital repositioning, planetary relocation, engineered energy sources and artificial system assembly; autonomous creation networks.
Research, discoveries and accomplishments jointly gate capabilities; no fixed playtime gates. Earlier work becomes faster through real capacity, technology and automation. No mandatory ending; finite configurable simulation budgets, not literal infinite scale.
## Core loop and controls
Discover → engineer → transform → populate → develop → defend → advance. Select target, project, priority and authority; autonomous fleets reserve resources and execute phases. Inspect progress, pause/cancel, change priority, take direct RTS control or delegate. Visible terrain and infrastructure changes persist. Advanced planet/species/system design is optional.
## Twelve integrated additions
- Great Filter: causal stellar instability, ecological collapse, divergence, fragmentation, engineering failure, scarcity, rival breakthroughs and displacement; actionable alerts, no arbitrary punishment.
- Advanced stellar engineering: late fictional capabilities with resource, energy, stability and logistics constraints.
- Civilization seeding: primordial, ecological, intelligent, primitive, industrial, advanced and hybrid starting profiles; observe, protect or influence development.
- Intelligent species design: traits affect mobility, environmental access, construction, economy and military equipment via validated modular content, not arbitrary bonuses.
- Living Civilization Tree: lineage and historical branches in World Archive.
- Protectorates: independent, protected, affiliated, research partner, federation, administered or rival relationships; autonomy and changing consent/relations modeled.
- Creator's Legacy: actual transformed worlds, species, civilizations, discoveries, reconstruction and victories feed existing achievements and records.
- Engineering fleets: surface, atmospheric, orbital, stellar, fabrication, ecology, reconstruction, seeding, research and command archetypes; faction-specific templates.
- Dynamic engineering campaigns: First Atmosphere, Great Restoration, Stellar Frontier, Cradle of Life, First Civilization, Broken Ring, Vhessar Incursion, Wandering World, Beyond the Natural; generated from actual state.
- War of Architects: competition over energy, infrastructure, research, territory and protected civilizations; capture, defense, liberation, restoration and multi-domain warfare.
- Universal Engineering Library: reusable world, system, species, civilization, ecosystem, structure, fleet, compound and scenario templates through existing creator/Workshop.
- Evolution Observatory: actionable milestones, discoveries, threats and requests via existing intelligence/archive; jump to relevant location.
## Modes
Beyond Evolution Sandbox, Stellar Architects, Evolution Wars, First World, Civilization Experiment, Reconstruction, Artificial Frontier, Architect's Crucible, Evolution Theatre; First Makers, War of Architects and Living Experiment as curated presets within shared mode families, not separate engines. Procedural scenarios, configurable starts, persistent saves; sandbox exceptions clearly marked. Preserve existing mode and ownership restrictions.
