# DEAD SECTOR GDD

Owner-approved G18 design direction; implementation pending. Engineering contracts, candidate algorithms and schemas below are documentation-only proposals, not runtime code. Existing source, assets, saves and gate completion statuses are untouched.

## Approved decisions (11/11)
1 D: one unified mode, four scenario types mixing A–D.
2 D+: dedicated PATHOGEN only in these scenarios.
3 D+ horror: walkers/runners, specialists, environmental evolution, infected wildlife, rare strains and dormancy; recognizable silhouettes, credible limits, no fantasy/bullet-sponge filler.
4 D+ cinematic horror: low tones, dark atmosphere, tension, silence and dynamic score.
5 D: short, objective-based, endless and persistent survival; procedural histories.
6 D: existing legal factions/stages and military/civilian/advanced/collapsed/underground starts; machines not biologically infected, infrastructure vulnerable.
7 D: supply, injury, infection, morale, panic, fatigue, desertion and emergencies; automate logistics and medical management.
8 D: urban, wilderness and connected subterranean horror, persistent destruction.
9 D+: 2–4 initial online, shared or independent command, optional competition/alliances/betrayal, persistent saves, reconnect/AI replacement.
10 D: independent causal infection, no artificial punishment or unfair spawning.
11 D excluding individual soldier-follow: traditional RTS, cinematic zoom, fog/visibility, spatial audio, connected surface/underground. No first-person or soldier-follow.

## Scenarios and loop
| Scenario | Start and player goal | Options |
|---|---|---|
| Horde Defense | Defensive compound or survivor base; build, fortify, survive | time/wave/hold objective or endless; waves derived from actual populations |
| Outbreak Survival | Scattered forces or civilian settlement; scavenge, rescue, expand | extraction, secure zones, long-term civilization |
| Operation Containment | Military assets at initial outbreak or collapsed facility; contain, evacuate, investigate | quarantine, research, rescue, secure or extract objectives |
| Total Outbreak | Combined dynamic world; build, explore, fight, survive | persistent endless history or chosen victory |

Quick-start selects coherent map/seed, scenario, legal faction and challenge; optional customization starting stage, population, resources, initial strain/extent, environments, difficulty, scarcity, fog, teams, victory, mutators. Procedural and authored maps with valid routes, resources and counterplay. No permanent unlock grind. Short 30-minute target through open-ended long saves (not performance claim). Scavenge -> establish -> fortify -> discover -> respond -> expand; every action yields meaningful consequences and new options. Player uses existing selection, build/train/research, groups, move/attack, officer automation; no individual supply, trauma or medical UI chores. Automation covers evacuation, triage, repair, resupply, quarantine under directives and resources. Machines retain energy/infrastructure dependencies; no biological conversion for synthetic units. Human A exclusive UNDERSTRIKE construction and existing faction rules retained; DLC playable factions only in Skirmish.

## Horror combat and world
Abandoned cities, hospitals, industrial districts, military checkpoints, residences, remote roads, forests/mountains, laboratories, bunkers/tunnels and connected facilities. Actual lighting/power, fog, smoke, night vision/flashlights, directional visibility, sensor uncertainty and delayed communications. Hidden threats obey simulated knowledge and movement, not scripted teleportation. Infected populations migrate, hibernate, adapt and attack; survivors establish rival communities, refugees and rescue opportunities; infrastructure collapses and abandoned zones persist. Ammo/fuel/medical stores, injuries, equipment wear, morale, exhaustion, command breakdown and retreat; communicate at squad level, not individual micro. Death/infection/conversion consistent across saves. Escalation based on world conditions; player victory possible, safe areas can genuinely be safe if secured. Avoid repetitive jumpscares or fake events. Fear through uncertainty and authentic consequence.

## Cinematic audio and readability
Low-frequency orchestral beds, deep bass drones, distant vocalizations/screams, distorted radio, structural creaks, quiet melancholic music, natural silence, escalating percussion in major battles. Music mixes from observable environmental/incident state, not a hidden enemy radar or predictable wave clock. Smooth transitions, cooldown and limited voice counts; spatial attenuation/occlusion, underground reflections, power failure ambience. Distinct telegraphs for urgent threats, no music masking orders. Subtitles, visual equivalents, separate music/SFX/voice controls, low-frequency intensity option, reduced flashing/shake; no reliance on audio alone. Traditional isometric RTS art, readable silhouettes and terrain, cinematic zoom only, no soldier-follow. Surface/underground transitions share state, no camera advantage.

## Multiplayer and persistence
Solo offline every compatible scenario; online shared civilization with scoped roles or shared pool, allied independent factions, optional resource competition, alliances/betrayal only explicit lobby rules. 2–4 initial target; host authoritative PATHOGEN, same causal events for all, per-team fog; reconnect and AI replacement use shared networking; persistent scenario saves independent from Dynasty. No always-on world. Reuse existing simulation and [network](G18_ONLINE_MULTIPLAYER.md), [PATHOGEN](PATHOGEN_DIRECTOR.md); final art/audio/strain balancing and map inventory pending production. Scenario-specific playtests for enjoyment, tension, intuitive controls and meaningful counterplay.
