## Workstreams (candidate)
EX-A shared scenario/geometry/templates (G11–17); EX-B doctrine/operations/identity/archive (G12–17); EX-C five new mode vertical slices (G12–18); EX-D creator/editor/SDK (G12–18); EX-E replay/spectator/records (G12–18); EX-F player experience (G11–18); EX-G platform product/Workshop/Store/commerce (G18 after shared contracts); EX-H production/QA/benchmark/save/content/release (G11–18). Preserve existing G18-A/B/C/D, MP/PL/DS/SW workstreams. Reuse G18 transport and authority; all eleven base-game experiences and launch-critical systems required before base game acceptance. Prototype network/snapshot interface early within owner-approved scope; no parallel writes to shared contracts. Mode family vertical slice before scaling procedural content. Paid creator marketplace/public third-party storefront phased after policy and financial validation, not required for launch free store; chosen game/DLC commerce/entitlements and minimal developer integration are launch-critical.
