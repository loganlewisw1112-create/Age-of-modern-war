# PLAYER EXPERIENCE

Owner-approved expanded design direction; implementation pending. Candidate contracts and acceptance criteria are documentation only, not shipped capabilities.

## Simple default experience
Keep the established RTS control scheme, familiar HUD, low-detail readable visuals and direct intervention. Main menu groups Quick Play, existing/new modes, continue, creator, eligible online and settings; eleven base-game experiences discoverable, not eleven competing complex setup flows. Recommended presets by desired duration/experience, legal faction/stage and owned content. Advanced customization collapsed by default, saved favorites and explicit load warnings. No account for offline, no forced tutorial, battle pass or pop-up grind.

## Military Academy
Playable short exercises using actual RTS mechanics, not separate fake simulation. Basics (select, move, build, harvest, train, research, win), terrain/cover, combined arms, logistics/maintenance, officers/operations, aviation, naval, intelligence, underground legal mechanics, regional command, creator and online roles. Each has one objective, optional hints, reset/skip, completion and next suggested lesson. Unlock lessons by available content, not mandatory completion. Preserve existing tutorial; no campaign spoilers. Evaluate first-time player success using actual playtests.

## Contextual assistance and dashboard
When production, construction, route, order or operation stalls, show why (resource/technology/access/clearance/authority/queue) and one actionable option, not automatic destructive intervention. Reuse authoritative validator reason codes, observer scope and command bus. Optional assisted actions preview cost/scope; user confirms major changes. Automation modes manual/assisted/autonomous, simple priority controls; no forced micromanagement. Unified adaptable dashboard by tactical/operational/strategic view: forces, facilities, supply, objectives, emergencies, ongoing operations, filters, saved layouts, search by known entities, click to focus or issue permitted order. No all-knowing intel; hidden information stays hidden. UI does not own state.

Notification grouping by cause/formation/region, severity, cooldown and dedupe; one supply report instead of dozens of vehicle alerts. Urgent direct threat clearly visible, routine events grouped in optional log, acknowledge/mute by category, persistent warnings remain inspectable. Accessibility supports scalable UI/text, remappable keys, keyboard navigation, redundant team cues, reduced flash/shake, captions and visual audio equivalents, separate music/SFX/voice and low-frequency intensity. Preserve mode-specific original contracts; resolve conflicting historical options (e.g. dark visual presentation) through gate review, no silent removal of existing assets. Camera and effects never block orders; no individual soldier-follow in new modes.

## Encyclopedia, resume and personalization
Searchable discovered faction/unit/building/tech/doctrine/resource/environment/mechanics reference, concise cards and optional detail; legal prerequisites, counters and role hints from content data, no undiscovered spoilers. After-action summary explains objective outcome and causal evidence. Resume brief shows last completed events, important active operations and urgent state; offline time does not advance when closed. Personal statistics and records local by default; no online campaign telemetry. Favorites, presets, optional saved layouts, cinematic battle photography/replay presentation, hardware recommendations. Independent difficulty dimensions (AI competence, economy realism, pressure, assistance) and readable scenario recommendations. 
Tactical pause/order queue approved direction for compatible offline play at G11: pause, queue legal commands, preview, undo pending orders, resume and revalidate atomically; no retroactive effect, no state advance while paused. Preserve existing Last Protocol timing contract unless owner-reviewed mode change; online pause only consensual/host rules, no unilateral competitive pause. Existing speed/quality constraints resolved at gate contract, no change to battle outcomes by rendering settings.

## Acceptance
New player can start a suitable match quickly, learn essentials, identify/resolve blocked action and issue operation without manuals; experienced player can skip hints, customize and directly control. Readability in all four facings and underground transitions, keyboard/scale/color-safe tests, offline account-free, no spoilers, observer-scoped dashboard, grouped alerts under stress, tactical pause deterministic, save/resume accurate. Qualitative fun/usability playtests required; no numerical retention claims. G11 pause/accessibility, G12 Academy, G15/G17 archive/dashboard, G18 complete experience and creator/online flows.
