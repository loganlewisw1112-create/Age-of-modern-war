# G10.1 approved Coalition art

The owner approved all 31 Phase B assets in full on November 26, 2026. Both contact sheets are generated by the same script that exports the game sprites.

## Vehicles

- The five technicals share a compact utility chassis with a short nose, four wheel positions, a central cab and a rear equipment bed. The weapon silhouettes use a ring mount, a twin-tube carrier, a search head, a short elevated tube and a long tube, respectively.
- The captured tank and refit IFV use tracked hulls. Their long turret and compact sensor turret distinguish the heavy and support roles. The shock truck, stealth APC, recovery truck and carrier have longer wheelbases with distinct emitters, ramps, booms and trays.
- The Ghost Runner and Exo Mule use compact bodies with a sensor head or secured cargo. The four watercraft use a tapered bow, low deck and role-specific equipment. The mini-sub is identified by its low sail and periscope.

## Facilities

- The Safehouse keeps its residential massing in both states. A marker and entrance hardware identify it once revealed. Workshops and markets use low bays and awnings. Broker and patron facilities use taller envelopes.
- Concealed hatch surfaces show low ground cover and ventilation. Discovered entrances show a low armored collar, door seam and edge lights. Damaged states expose the access well while the building's underlying simulation state remains intact.
- The renderer selects the cover per player and preserves it until that player has reveal evidence. Building rotation, construction and damage use the existing battle-view variant selection.
