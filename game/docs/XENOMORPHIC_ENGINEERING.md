# Xenomorphic Engineering — G21
Fictional abstract game genetics, not real laboratory procedures. Extend XENOGENESIS Species Genesis, Biological Evolution, Ecology and Civilization Evolution after G17/G20 acceptance. No unrestricted species generation in earlier DLCs.
## Creation and progression
Discover and archive fictional traits; unlock modification, compatible hybridization, trait splicing, original species, synthetic and organic-machine hybrids, extinct lineage restoration and directed evolution. Optional editor or simple objective → proposed profile → compatibility/viability preview → resource and research validation → approval → seeding. Traits cover anatomy, appearance, locomotion, metabolism, lifespan, reproduction, senses, cognition, social behavior, environment tolerance, technology integration and evolutionary potential. Tradeoffs, compatibility and bounded variation prevent universal optimal species. Aggregate populations, not individual organisms.
## Civilization seeding
Seed primordial/ecological/intelligent populations or primitive/industrial/advanced/hybrid settlements. Choose homeworld, starting infrastructure, technology, relationship and broad doctrine. Independent civilizations develop economies, cities, politics, language, military and expansion; no guaranteed loyalty. Protectorate, affiliation, research partnership, federation, administration and rivalry use existing diplomacy. World Archive tracks ancestry, divergence, discoveries and historical events. Myrieme Forge generates versioned offline language packages without changing established canon.
## RTS content
Validated modular assets and templates map traits to movement, environment access, construction, equipment, units and buildings. Generated factions need reachable production, logistics, economy, AI orders and coherent doctrine before activation. Use stable generated IDs and seed/version; preserve definitions in saves. No external generation service. Player may observe or intervene within existing mode rules.
## Acceptance
Create and merge two compatible fictional species, reject invalid profiles, seed populations, observe deterministic bounded divergence, generate a functioning civilization with military and economy, preserve lineage and language across saves, validate independent diplomacy and conflict. Sandbox imports cannot bypass normal campaign prerequisites.
