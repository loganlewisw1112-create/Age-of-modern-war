# WORLD ARCHIVE

Owner-approved expanded design direction; implementation pending. Proposed IDs, interfaces and pseudocode are design artifacts only. Reuse the Godot/Rust simulation, current director scheduler, validators and event ledger.

## Shared historical projection
Extend the Historical & Generational Director's existing history, named personnel and institutions into a read-oriented archive service. Not an additional director, not a second simulation, no authoritative state mutation. Consume validated event ledger and stable identity references. Record events once by stable ID; indexes rebuilt from checkpoint+events if needed. Keep bounded summaries for distant history while retaining lineage, important causal references and consequential outcomes. No mandatory player data entry.

Formation identity: stable ID, optional name/insignia, founding tick/date, parent/lineage IDs, current commander, branch, status, service summary, battles, honors and aggregate casualties. Rename/cosmetic insignia optional and validated; automatic defaults. Split creates new linked ID; merge records lineage and retires predecessor or preserves designated successor under explicit rule. Replacement/reorganization changes roster without erasing institutional identity. Retired/destroyed status permanent until legitimate successor, no resurrection. A company can endure across generations with different people and equipment. Detail optional, one card for normal play.

Archive views: battle record, service record, timeline, civilization milestones, commanders, discoveries, memorials, regional change, world events, campaign chapters, achievements, replay links. Retain chronological tick and world date, cause references, affected entity/region IDs, visibility/entitlement, summary and version. No spoilers: only discovered or permitted evidence; online per-team visibility, post-match revelation policy explicit. No global profile upload from offline Inheritance/Living World/Dynasty; offline history private. User-created replay exports only eligible modes and permitted data, never automatic cloud sync. Archive entries may be summarized but important events and identity remain stable; cap size measured for long saves.

Candidate event projection: Event {id, tick, category, subject_refs, causal_refs, visibility, payload_version}; ArchiveIndex {save_id, event_cursor, subject, summaries, replay_ref}; Honor {id, rule_version, qualifying_event_ids, formation_id, earned_tick}. Deterministic projection and deduplication; score/award events come from authorized achievement evaluator not UI. Records distinguish real simulation outcomes from authored scenario initial history; no fake accomplishments. UI offers optional after-action and resume brief, compare timeline and inspect records. No extra notifications for routine losses; grouped reports. Export permitted snapshots locally, preserve version and provenance. Tests: lineage across split/merge and save/migration, event dedupe, archive rebuild, visibility/spoilers, offline privacy, generation transitions, long-run size, replay/achievement references.
