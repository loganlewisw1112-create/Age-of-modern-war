# g6_infantry notes (art-infantry)
Module renders through `bash run.sh g6_infantry`: 16/16 plates. Squads use the G5 figure kit: FIG styles and poses, SQ.std/fig_at/prop_at/layered, SL.squad_sheet.

- a_marines: squad 4, 48x36. Frames idle walk_a walk_b fire wade wounded. In `wade` the men walk line-abreast through a surf patch, with their legs cut at the waterline (foam ring) and chest spray. Coyote-olive coats, team helmets.
- a_paratroopers: squad 4, 48x36. Frames idle walk_a walk_b fire(kneel_aim) land wounded. In `land` one man is still under a gored canopy (with his landing shadow), one is down with a half-collapsed chute, and two kneel to secure. Check that the canopy stays inside the frame top at every facing.
- a_eod: squad 2, 48x36. Frames idle walk_a walk_b fire sweep wounded. Built from ex.gd's a_eod_sappers. `sweep` shows the detector over a dug-out AT mine, red lane pins and white tape, and the partner kneeling at the mine.
- a_jtac: squad 2, 48x36. Frames idle walk_a walk_b fire lase wounded. In `lase` the controller kneels behind a tripod designator, a pulsed red beam runs to a reticle on the target, and the RTO kneels with the handset.
- a_manpads: squad 2, 48x36. Frames idle walk_a walk_b fire aim wounded. `fire` is the launch: the tube is raised on the shoulder, with backblast and a corkscrew smoke trail to the missile. `aim` is tracking with the spotter on binoculars.
- b_conscripts: squad 10 on its own big_sheet, 64x44 with pivot 32,35 and alive_sheets 1..9. Frames idle walk_a walk_b fire charge wounded. Kit is mixed (caps and helmets, shovels and picks, a pistol commissar). `charge` is a ragged surge with the commissar's arm raised and a team-dyed banner. The renderer must accept fw/fh other than 48x36 for this squad.
- b_satchel_sappers: squad 3, 48x36. Frames idle walk_a walk_b fire(crouch_aim) plant wounded. In `plant` the lead kneels at a brick wall section, sets a satchel with a lit fuse (sparks and smoke), one man covers against the wall and one sprints back. SHOW uses row 6, because the wall hides the planter at camera-facing rows. Standard frames show hip satchels.
- b_marksman: squad 1, 48x36. Frames idle walk_a walk_b fire prone wounded. `prone` and `fire` show a long scoped rifle on a bipod with hessian sacking over the back. `prone` has a scope glint and `fire` has a flash. The figure is small, like the shipped sniper, since it is a single man.
- b_propaganda_team: squad 2, 48x36. Frames idle walk_a walk_b fire broadcast wounded. The bannerman always carries a team-dyed banner and the operator has a horn on his radio pack. `broadcast` adds a twin-horn mast with a cable to the kneeling operator at the mic and sound rings. SHOW row 0.
- m_shield_drone: veh/air, 64x60 pivot 32,28, altitude 16. Frames idle move fire shield. A hex Machine drone with 4 rotor pods and a forward emitter. `fire` shows a partial wall and `shield` a full curved translucent cyan wall from the ground up, with lattice nodes and deflection sparks. The wall stays out of the body pixels at facings where it is behind the drone (_overlay).
- m_stalker_synth: veh, 36x44. Frames idle walk_a walk_b fire cloak. A tall digitigrade synth with a monoblade arm and a needle carbine. `fire` is the slash arc. `cloak` is a refracting ghost (alpha ~0.12, with shimmer edge, seams and eye kept). Wreck: collapsed.
- m_repair_swarm2: veh, 64x52. Frames idle move fire work. Two-tier node with an uplink mast and 6 bots (g5_units_m bots). In `work`, repair beams and a dashed data relink from the mast go into a cold damaged Machine hulk whose seams are half re-lit.
- m_breacher_crawler: veh, 44x40. Frames idle move fire detonate. A 6-legged crawler with a panelled shaped-charge cone and standoff probe. `fire` is armed (white-hot face). `detonate` shows the forward jet, fireball, debris and smoke.
- a_exo_trooper: veh, 40x44 (1 man, about twice rifleman height). Frames idle walk_a walk_b fire. P.box powered armour in VH.HULL olive with team shoulder pads and pack top, a VISION visor and a rotary gun on the right arm with ammo belt. `fire` adds a flash and ejected brass. SHOW row 2, where the gun reads. Wreck: suit lying burnt.
- x_ambush_cell: squad 4, 48x36. Frames idle walk_a walk_b fire hidden ambush wounded. Mismatched kit: tan, captured olive, grey and sand coats, jeans, red and black headscarves (scarf post-process on the figure), a captured B helmet, a cap, and an RPG. `hidden`/`ambush` put the cell in double brush clumps with a rubble stub, and `ambush` fires with RPG backblast.
- x_tunnel: building size 1, top 22, rotations f0/f1. Features: a dark shaft with a ladder, a timber frame with a galvanised lean-to and camo net, a sandbag horseshoe, a fresh spoil heap, brush, a team rag on a stick, and a scarfed RPG fighter climbing out (waist cut at the pit mouth). The lean-to roof reads weakly at 1x.
