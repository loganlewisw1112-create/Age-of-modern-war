# g6_seaair (art-seaair): 10 ships + 4 aircraft, all vehicle sheets (8 facings + wreck)
Ships: convex hull prisms shaded like P.box (side_shade + speckle), g5_bld_sea hull colours, translucent wake / waterline foam / bow spray painted only outside the hull. Frame fitted to all live frames.
- a_supply_ship_a: ship; idle/move/fire/work; ~109x77. Containers, 2 fuel tanks, crane, aft bridge, team hull plate + funnel band. work = crane swings container out + fuel hose over the side.
- b_supply_ship_b: ship; idle/move/fire/work; ~107x93. Riveted rust hull, 3 round primer tanks, wheelhouse with team roof, sooty stack. work = hose.
- m_charge_tender: catamaran; idle/move/fire/work; ~96x67. Twin graphite hulls with cyan seams, 6 battery cells, pylon eye. work = cyan charge arc to the side.
- a_minesweeper: ship; idle/move/fire/sweep; ~100x60. Bow 20mm, A-frame, cable reel, orange floats. sweep = cable + float trailing astern.
- b_mine_barge: barge; idle/move/fire/lay; ~103x74. Two mine rails with horned contact mines, forward wheelhouse. lay = mine dropping off the stern with splash.
- a_hovercraft: air-cushion craft; idle/move/fire/loaded/unload; ~103x69. Rubber skirt, side cabins, 2 ducted fans; loaded = tank in the well; unload = bow ramp down, tank rolling out. Plate on water|beach.
- b_aa_barge: barge; idle/move/fire; ~105x78. 3 riveted flak tubs with twin guns at different bearings; fire = flashes + flak puffs.
- m_lance_cruiser: warship; idle/move/fire; ~144x98. Sharp graphite hull, hex superstructure, centreline twin rail lance; fire = cyan bolt from the rail tip.
- a_carrier: carrier; idle/move/fire/launch; ~140x93. Angled flight deck with markings, starboard island (team band), 6 parked jets; launch = jet on catapult with afterburner + steam; fire = CIWS tracers.
- a_submarine: sub; idle/move/fire/dive; ~137x94. Low dark hull awash, sail with team band; fire = torpedo track; dive = periscope + ghost hull only.
Aircraft: g5_air_mesh models rendered with g5_air_raster (G5 aircraft pipeline, faction styles a/b/m) inside SL.veh_sheet; manifest air keys (air, altitude 34, ground_frames, shadow blob). SHOW alt 26 + shadow.
- b_recon_plane: idle/move/fire/idle_b/move_b/landing/parked; ~80x76. Rusty high-wing single prop, wing struts, fixed gear, belly camera pod; fire = camera flash.
- a_awacs: same frames; ~112x95. 4-engine jet with team-banded rotodome; fire = radar pulse rings.
- a_airlift: + drop; ~145x103. 4-turboprop high-wing cargo plane; drop = ramp open, pallet under a chute (plate row 3).
- m_stealth_drone: same frames as recon; ~84x61. Graphite flying wing, sawtooth trailing edge, cyan exhaust slots, team wingtips; fire = bay open + missile.
