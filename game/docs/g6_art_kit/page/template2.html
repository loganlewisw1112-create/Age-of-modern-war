Section 2 · Why these proposals

Six gaps after G5

Each proposal card is tagged with the gap it closes (G1 to G6).

G1 · Machine falls behind

1 new ground unit vs 15 for the humans; no warship; no new infantry tier.

G2 · Human A has no artillery

Its artillery role slot is empty until Guided Artillery at Stage 9. Only the Mortar Team covers it.

G3 · Human B recon and air holes

Its recon role is empty, and so is its Stage 9 air role.

G4 · Thin defense

One tower, one wall tower and one coastal battery each. No AT, AA or artillery emplacements, no mines. Cover comes only from hills.

G5 · No supply ship

The naval rules say ships can restock "from a supply ship", but no such unit exists in naval.json.

G6 · No counter-drone layer

Drones are on every side now. Only the EMP drone and the SPAAG answer them.

Provenance

The roster and gap counts were read from game/content/*.json, content/g4, content/g5 and factions.json on 2026-10-24. Section 1 shows the repo's own G5 sheets. Every proposal plate was rendered headless with Godot 4.7.2 from drawer scripts that call the game's tools/art libraries. Those drawers live outside the repo; nothing was written into the game folder. Stage suggestions are proposals. No stats, costs or balance are implied.
