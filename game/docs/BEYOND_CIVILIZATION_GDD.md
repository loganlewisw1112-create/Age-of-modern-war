# BEYOND CIVILIZATION — owner-approved master DLC design
Status: approved design, 2026-11-27; not implemented, not a frozen gate. Read [Campaign Vision](CAMPAIGN_VISION.md), [Next-generation GDD](NEXTGEN_SIMULATION_GDD.md), [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md), [XENOGENESIS](XENOGENESIS.md), [Myrieme Forge](MYRIEME_FORGE_INTEGRATION.md), [Universe and civilizations](INTERSTELLAR_UNIVERSE.md) and [delivery plan](DLC_DELIVERY_PLAN.md).

## Product contract
Offline single-player expansion of the existing Godot/Rust RTS. Inspiration: Elite Dangerous, Starfield, No Man's Sky and the expansive outer-system atmosphere of Star Wars Outer Rim; original setting, not borrowed IP. Build, discover, acquire, fight, upgrade, expand. Deep simulation is invisible by default. Selection, right-click, groups, build/train/research and direct tactical intervention remain the primary controls. Delegate routine work; no mandatory language puzzles, piloting, docking, refueling, scanning, taxes, population administration, component engineering or diplomatic paperwork. Avoid repetitive quests, forced notifications and artificial punishment for success. Every system must demonstrate gameplay value or be simplified.

## Expansion boundary and continuity
UNDERSTRIKE is a planned unimplemented base-game addition, not DLC. DLC 1 (Space Expansion, existing G18) unlocks one home star system: Earth, orbit, Moon and further planets/moons, stations, shipyards, belts, colonies and existing arrival war. Preserve all existing authored aerospace milestones and six hull rosters (Human A 21, Human B 22, Machine 24, Coalition 22, Vhessar 21, Unbound 19); no additional DLC. DLC 2 (Beyond Civilization, working title) opens remaining 49 systems across seven authored regions, including four further Core systems. Finite universe, no procedural expansion beyond boundary. Existing G19 remains intact; no new numbered gate assigned. Ownership unlocks access, not free technology or resources. Continue existing save; base campaign outcome directly sets player assets, surviving factions, territorial control, tech, alliances, rivalries, infrastructure and starting crises. Preserve Purge/Restore/Merge/Fall and arrival Repel/Viceroy/Alliance/Lost outcomes and their consequences; no story rewrite. Existing 13 stages remain unchanged; aerospace/interstellar capability milestones separate. Campaign personally played battle rule remains; delegation of battles in Living World/skirmish, routine assistance in campaign. New DLC factions fully playable upon ownership in Skirmish only; never switch to them in Campaign/Living World. AI encounters, trade, salvage and alliances permitted.

## Gameplay loops
Explore: select destination, dispatch fleet, investigate interesting signals or delegate. Travel uses validated propulsion/range, supply and routes; contact can change plans, no forced minigame. Colonize: select suitable site and development priority (resource, industry, defense, science, civilian or balanced); validated construction, crews and logistics execute. Discover: research artifacts automatically, compete for sites, unlock meaningful capabilities. Warfare: issue defend/invade/conquer orders, assemble forces, coordinate orbital and surface actions, intercept supplies, capture or destroy infrastructure; jump into tactical RTS anywhere supported. Diplomacy: clear relations and high-level offers; AI negotiations constrained by knowledge, history and resources. Research: choose tech or broad priority, auto allocate; foreign tech requires compatible tooling, materials and knowledge. Engineering: place project, fund and supply, delegate construction; inspect or defend site. No mandatory micro for any loop.

## Skirmish
Owned faction rosters immediately unlocked; no campaign gate. Existing customizer expanded with legal map/domain combinations, independent stages/tech/resources/team/difficulty, wildlife, events and victory. Presets Classic RTS, Alien Invasion, First Contact, Planetary Conquest, Fleet Warfare, Civilization Wars, Survival Outpost and Total Sandbox. Save scenarios; optional advanced settings; quick launch. Major and minor factions require full coherent playable roster where introduced as playable; historical precursor remnants not automatically a playable faction. No unsupported start quietly substituted.

## Approved finalization components
1. Five initial major powers (two existing + three new), four minor/emerging concepts, one precursor; all eight archetypes covered; minors can rise. Canon names pending Forge and review.
2. Seven regions, fifty-system baseline including home; fixed stellar layout, evolving politics, varied density; exact catalog/coordinates/planet inventories pending.
3. Full Forge production/runtime across both DLCs; all 15 engines and 8 adapters; universal integration where useful.
4. One XENOGENESIS director, 20 logical subsystems; shared authoritative state with existing four.
5. Full documentation, dependency-driven delivery, acceptance gates; protect current base-game work.

All designs above approved; specific names, faction unit definitions, system inventories, engineering budgets and content production remain pending. No implementation or testing is implied.


## 2026-11-27 — Authoritative expansion integration

DLC 1 = Space Expansion (G18); DLC 2 = Beyond Civilization (future unnumbered). UNDERSTRIKE is integrated base game. True Dynasty is the primary earned progression from Inheritance through Living World and both expansions, preserving the same civilization history. Independent preset/custom starts in either owned DLC are separate saves, never modifying Dynasty; DLC 1 standalone can continue to DLC 2. New DLC factions playable only in Skirmish. Existing campaign battle rules, endings, arrival war, rosters and current gate statuses are protected. All new work is planned, not implemented.

DLC 2 inherits all consequential DLC 1 state: colonies, fleets, commanders, research, artifacts, activated mysteries, delayed signals, political relationships and conflicts. Independent starts initialize compatible history in separate saves. Existing 50-system scope, six gameplay phases and all existing rosters remain.
