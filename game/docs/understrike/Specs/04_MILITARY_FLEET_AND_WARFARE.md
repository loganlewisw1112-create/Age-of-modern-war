# 04 — UNDERSTRIKE military fleet, specialized units and combat doctrine

## Asymmetric ownership
Modern Human alone unlocks persistent bunker construction and manufacturing of the proprietary **MB 12-class armored boring vehicle family**. Capturing a vehicle/factory may offer salvage/use subject to compatibility, but does not grant a competing civilization the engineering branch. Enemies can deploy conventional infantry/robots, temporary tunneling assault apparatus and raids, preserving meaningful asymmetric counterplay.

## Proposed 12-vehicle family — working names/designations
All twelve classes are **approved as a design direction and distinct roles**, while physical dimensions, armaments and exact balance statistics remain engineering/creative proposals requiring playtesting.

Each class needs a production spec sheet: chassis physical envelope (width/height/turning), excavation cross-section, material compatibility, power and thermal budget, crew/automation needs, passenger/cargo capacity, protection zones, speed by geological medium, detection signature, signature-reduction modes, machinery component health, maintenance, build recipe, tech prerequisites, upgrades, vulnerabilities and visual state transitions. No arbitrary 'dig instantly anywhere' action.

## Specialized forces and equipment
- **Geological Corps:** survey engineers; automated survey rovers; borehole scanners; exploration drones; hazardous-material teams.
- **Military Excavation:** excavation rigs; engineering crawlers; reinforcement vehicles; debris haulers; mobile ventilation and repair units.
- **Subterranean Infantry:** tunnel infantry, reconnaissance scouts, specialized breach teams, combat engineers, medics and heavy defensive infantry.
- **Robotics:** compact underground reconnaissance units, autonomous defensive platforms, utility and rescue robots.
- **Command:** underground engineers, security officers, assault commanders and logistics leadership; personnel training/experience and equipment quality alter mission outcomes.

These are unit **families**, not promises that every listed unit exists in phase 1. Start with a playable combined-arms counter-set before expanding into full variants.

## Battlefield principles
1. Combat uses real available spaces. A force must have access, a fitting route, legal movement and supply; no teleportation.
2. Passage geometry is tactical. Narrow turns, chamber volumes, vertical shafts and damaged corridors change available formations and vehicle routes.
3. Sensor reports are uncertain. Detection derives from reconnaissance, geology, acoustic/vibration signature and communications, not omniscience.
4. Units consume ammunition/energy/fuel and can lose systems individually: cutter, propulsion, sensors, armor, power, passenger module.
5. Underground environmental threats matter: flooding, smoke, power loss, unstable structures and limited visibility may alter safe movement and combat orders.
6. Multi-floor capture is sector-by-sector; battle state, civilians, utility infrastructure and ownership persist.
7. The player uses classic select-and-order controls or assigns an officer a single mission with constraints. AI executes unit tactics without constant babysitting.

## Surface breakthrough
`Bore To Surface` is a special mission with route clearance, surface landing footprint, geological and urban/structure restrictions, concealment options, power/time constraints and appropriate unit envelope. On emergence: rock fractures/ground opens, the borer breaches, optional troops/vehicles deploy, new aperture becomes traversable and remains persistent. Reusable concealed exits require separate construction/resources/closure machinery. Enemy reconnaissance or seismic networks may warn of imminent breaches; a proposed countermeasure must be discoverable and fair. Surface and underground pathgraphs and ownership update atomically.

## Programmable hybrid-material engineering
Research allows advanced models to choose route blueprints (`Reinforced Passage`, `Military Road`, `Special Ops Tunnel`, `Utility Corridor`, `Heavy Rail Interface`, `Fortified Sector`, `Large Chamber`). Machinery automatically selects installable structural systems from owned recipes and available stock, with real material demand and staged fabrication. Experimental material properties require prior discovery, analysis and stable fabrication capabilities. No magical instant mass creation.

## Custom vehicle design
Intuitive base model → compatible slots and tradeoffs (chassis, cutters, armor, drive, power, sensors, payload, tools, specialized modules) → preview mobility/detectability/cost → Save variant → Manufacture/Retrofit. Enforce mass, dimension, cooling, energy, manufacturing and research constraints. AI can propose legal variants but never redesign the player's production blueprint without authorization.

## Formation AI
Order levels: Unit → Squad → Sub-command → Underground Task Force → Theater. Commander mission template: objective, route preference, ROE/risk level, available units, permissible expenditure, civilian protection rule, fallback/resupply priorities, reporting frequency and completion condition. Commander must not cross an explicit player order guardrail. Report success/failure reasons through concise after-action summaries.

## Combat balance matrix to prototype
Small reconnaissance borers outrun/hear less than heavy units but cannot tank a heavy engagement. Heavy boring units enable large-route access but are slow/detectable. Infantry dominate some corridors but lack protection in large exposed chambers. Autonomous robots resist some hazards but depend on power/control resilience. Defense in depth and blocked logistics can defeat an otherwise strong attacking force. Balance every offensive tactic with available counterplay at appropriate stage and a way to recognize the threat.
