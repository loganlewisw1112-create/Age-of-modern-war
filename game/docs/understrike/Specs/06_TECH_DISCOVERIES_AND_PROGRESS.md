# 06 — 13-stage technology, rare discoveries and underground exploration

## Technology unlock philosophy
A civilization stage indicates verified productive/scientific capability, not a blanket numerical combat buff. Surface and bunker research use the same authoritative stage and tech system. Accessing a deposit or salvaged piece of equipment does not automatically unlock its mass-production recipe. Modular industry, qualified personnel, materials, applied research and operating infrastructure are prerequisites.

## Proposed stage bands (not fixed unlock prices)
| Stage | Illustrative UNDERSTRIKE progression |
| --- | --- |
| 1–4 | No constructible major bunker; ordinary survival shelters/caves may exist as map content |
| 5–6 | Early underground shelters, storage, mining, survey and industrial support |
| 7 | Mechanized military excavation, entry bunkers, initial hardened sectors, conventional freight |
| 8 | Substantial modern military bunkers, concealed vehicle lift, modern underground logistics |
| 9 | Networked sensors, advanced boring fleets, underground warfare command, specialized automation |
| 10 | Autonomous mining and logistics, high-efficiency construction and military deployment |
| 11 | Advanced composites, experimental programmable tunnel fabrication, synthetic materials |
| 12 | Distributed underground cities, sophisticated adaptive engineering, long-range network redundancy |
| 13 | Apex material engineering and elite/high-throughput subterranean capabilities |

Individual tech paths (fortification, excavation speed, stealth, sensors, transport, emergency resilience, manufacturing, advanced materials) can be specialized independently within stages. Player can pursue bunker development without making all stage-appropriate tools automatic; no punitive player-level drill-down.

## Resource classification
- **Common/industrial:** iron/copper-bearing ores, stone, carbon feedstocks, usable structural spoil.
- **Strategic/rare:** enriched deposits, useful rare-element formations, specialized alloy inputs.
- **Extremely rare:** distinctive unusual materials supporting narrow high-value tech paths.
- **Anomalous:** samples of uncertain composition/origin, requiring study and safe handling.

Rarity must be **geology- and scenario-dependent**, not unconditionally tied to floor depth. Depth may alter the statistical distribution; certain discoveries can remain surface-adjacent or trapped in ancient human installations. Unique discoveries must not be required to complete the standard 13-stage economic or victory path.

## Four approved origin categories
1. Extraterrestrial origin (meteorite or other off-world source).
2. Lost pre-collapse human technology (industrial composites, experimental laboratories, advanced alloys).
3. Ancient non-human civilization (buried manufactured structures, machines, exotic compounds).
4. Unknown / unresolved (not everything receives a tidy answer).

A material may have a **hidden true origin** in scenario data. Player-visible identity evolves from unknown → tentative identification → supported analysis as samples/research improve. Avoid exposing hidden-origin spoilers in the normal HUD.

## Discovery pipeline
Survey anomaly → investigate at appropriate hazard level → collect credible sample → research classification → identify extractable portions → acquire tooling → extract and transport physical quantities → process in qualified facility → unlock specialized recipe or knowledge. Costs and discoveries persist; conflicts over deposits emerge naturally through territorial control.

Natural geology includes caverns, underground lakes/rivers, mineral concentrations, geothermal zones, faults and unusual rock layers. Ancient and artificial finds include buried facilities, nonhuman structures and research ruins. Decision 19 approved **geology + ancient/non-human**, not every optional dynamic threat or expansive story-quest branch proposed in the more ambitious alternative. Introduce optional threats or expeditions only if they serve existing approved systems and are not treated as mandatory.

## Discovery result examples (illustrative only)
- A hard-to-process alloy permits better bunker reinforcement after sufficient fabrication research.
- A geothermal reservoir makes local energy independence more feasible but raises drilling/heat management challenges.
- An anomalous sample leads to a specialized tunneling sensor, not a globally upgraded army.
- Ancient ruins become preservable reference sites, extraction zones or blocked geological terrain based on discovery rules.

## Anti-power-creep balancing
Rare technology should grant a **new strategic option with an opportunity cost**, not a guaranteed win. Restrict by production capacity, expensive refining, limited volumes, specialist availability, realistic counters and time to deploy. Make common-resource alternatives viable at the same overall civilization stage.
