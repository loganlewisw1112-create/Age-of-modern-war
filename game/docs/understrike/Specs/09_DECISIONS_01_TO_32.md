## Inheritance/exception notes
- A D-level selection is design intent for **full game**, not a requirement that first playable delivers the entire feature.
- Decisions 11, 19, 21, 22 and 23 were deliberate narrower/hybrid selections, and must NOT be silently converted to the longest D option.
- Decision 28 is a correction to an earlier assistant proposal granting enemy factions bunker construction. The correction wins absolutely.
- Subterranean industrial and combat vehicle names MB-01..12 are **working designations** pending content/art validation; class differentiation and Modern Human exclusivity are binding.
- User's explicit quality bar throughout: believable mechanical functionality, natural proportions, polished animation/explosions, beautiful isometric presentation, substantial depth behind concise controls and no worker/resource micromanagement.
