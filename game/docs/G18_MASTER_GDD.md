# G18 MASTER GDD

Owner-approved G18 design direction; implementation pending. Engineering contracts, candidate algorithms and schemas below are documentation-only proposals, not runtime code. Existing source, assets, saves and gate completion statuses are untouched.

## Final base-game scope
G17 closes only after all 20 XENOGENESIS subsystems and all 15 Myrieme Forge engines/eight adapters and offline runtime pass acceptance. G18 completes the entire base game; G19 Space Expansion (DLC 1) and G20 Beyond Civilization (DLC 2) follow, in order. No additional DLC. Underlying UNDERSTRIKE is an integrated Human A branch in Inheritance and Living World (and compatible scenarios); SUBTERRANEAN WARFARE is a separate base-game scenario mode reusing it, not an expansion. Inheritance and Living World are strictly offline solo, including Dynasty continuation; no shared campaign saves, leaderboards or online authentication. The DLC civilization experiences remain offline solo; compatible owned DLC content extends Skirmish multiplayer, not mandatory online persistent worlds. DLC factions playable only in Skirmish, preserving existing ownership/canon restrictions.

## Four original coordinated tracks and expanded workstreams
G18-A: shared multiplayer, Last Protocol and Skirmish online; G18-B: independently controlled open-source platform; G18-C: DEAD SECTOR four horror scenarios and PATHOGEN; G18-D: SUBTERRANEAN WARFARE five scenarios and DEEPWAR. Both directors are mode-exclusive, one authoritative simulation, no duplicate engine, no always-running AI. Names and track IDs working names. All 11 DEAD SECTOR and nine SUBTERRANEAN decisions approved; see individual documents.

The original G18-A/B/C/D scope remains mandatory; EX-A–H shared systems, five additional modes, creator ecosystem and production qualification extend it. See [expanded delivery](EXPANDED_GAMEPLAY_DELIVERY_PLAN.md).

## Gameplay contract
Select, right-click, build, train, research, attack, defend, delegate, intervene. Quick presets and optional advanced customizer; no individual soldier-follow camera in either new mode, no mandatory individual logistics/medical/engineering work. No new required game progression or grind. Deep simulation, exciting short operations and long persistent saves; causes not artificial punishment. Preserve core rendering style and low-detail RTS readability. Reuse existing assets/interfaces only where appropriate, original horror/warfare content and audio as needed. Performance budgets measured; no speculative maximum counts.

## Director activation
Four general directors (Dynamic World, Geopolitical, Historical & Generational, Faction Evolution) remain shared where relevant. XENOGENESIS fifth, fully implemented by G17, extraterrestrial simulation active only in entitled relevant content. PATHOGEN sixth and DEEPWAR seventh are separate G18 mode-specific policy engines. Neither active in Inheritance/Living World/ordinary Skirmish/Last Protocol; no automatic cross-mode activation even when a bunker exists. Each submits bounded validated intents to existing world ledger. Audio presentation consumes observable events, never mutates authoritative state. Shared services do not imply all seven run concurrently.
