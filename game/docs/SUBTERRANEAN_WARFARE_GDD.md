# SUBTERRANEAN WARFARE GDD

Owner-approved G18 design direction; implementation pending. Engineering contracts, candidate algorithms and schemas below are documentation-only proposals, not runtime code. Existing source, assets, saves and gate completion statuses are untouched.

## Five scenario types
| Scenario | Core gameplay | Typical objectives |
|---|---|---|
| Bunker Defense | Establish and fortify facilities, respond to incursions | hold sectors, protect infrastructure, survive |
| Underground Assault | Recon, breach and capture enemy facilities | secure target, disrupt military network, extract |
| Underground Empire | Build connected military-industrial cities and transport | economic/territorial supremacy |
| Combined Arms | Coordinate surface and subterranean battles | control approaches and underground objectives |
| Total Underground War | Combine all scenarios into persistent dynamic conflict | endless or chosen victory |

Use existing UNDERSTRIKE foundation, 15-floor architecture, modular rooms, concealed elevators, transport hubs, geology, structural state, power/air/water, military fleet, logistics, faction restrictions and surface links. No separate engine, standalone DLC or modification of Inheritance/Living World branch. Quick 20-minute operation target through persistent campaigns; no guaranteed scale. Authored/procedural layouts with reachable objectives, valid clearance/support, resources and faction-legal starts. Non-Human A starts may raid/occupy pre-existing facilities, not acquire protected construction rights. Custom starting stage, forces, facilities, resources, map seed, geology, hazards, fog, AI intelligence/aggression/doctrine, teams, alliance rules, optional objectives, victory/extraction. Quick launch valid preset; advanced controls optional; share settings/seed, not executable mods. In-match progression only.

## Engaging RTS loop
Build → discover → breach → conquer → expand → defend; different scenarios start at different steps. Unexpected breakthroughs and counteroffensives arise from real construction and intel, not teleported forces. Maintain usable player command (select, order, build, train, attack, research, delegate). Player sets sector, objective, priorities; officers handle excavation, resupply, repairs, reinforcement, evacuation and defensive response. Take over and resume current state at any time; no worker-by-worker or floor-by-floor chores. Meaningful weak points and counterplay, not repeated identical assaults. Multi-floor RTS navigation, cutaway and surface transitions, persistent geometry independent of camera. Concealed enemy routes require recon, limited knowledge, credible time/resources to create. Power failure, smoke, collapse, flooding and severed routes affect combat/industry; repair and reconstruction conserve costs.

## Atmosphere and multiplayer
Emergency lighting, dust/smoke, power states, spatial combat audio and military music scale with observable battle tension; legible warnings and audio alternatives. No soldier-follow or first-person in this mode. Existing separate underground survivor view in other authorized contexts not removed. DEEPWAR coordinates autonomous enemy and neutral operations, not forced player choices. Offline solo; online co-op attack/defense, competitive symmetrical/asymmetrical, alliances and shared/independent roles, persistent scenario saves and reconnect AI, public/private lobbies. Initial 2–4 target subject to validation. No cross-merge into campaign. Existing multiplayer faction restrictions and DLC-only Skirmish playability apply.
