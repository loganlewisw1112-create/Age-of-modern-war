# G10 Ghost Wing — owner-frozen look bible

**Owner freeze (2026-11-23).** This is the art and visual-read contract for the G10 Ghost Wing. It is subordinate to
`G10_SCOPE.md` for feature scope and does not close or approve G10 implementation.

## Faction read

Ghost Wing combines pre-war black operations, scavenged luxury and illegal hush-tech. It must not read as Machine
glow, Human olive-drab or a conventional national army.

- **Palette:** void charcoal, bruised purple-black, cold-bone trim and one patron-gold debt pinstripe.
- **Finish:** camo is an oil-slick/heat-shimmer distortion, never chrome. At rest it almost erases edges; sprinting,
  firing or taking a hit tears the shimmer and exposes the silhouette.
- **Cleanliness:** ordinary Coalition cells are ragged, field-repaired and mismatched. Ghost Wing is unnervingly
  clean: sealed ports, controlled seams and deliberate damage repair.
- **Team colour:** a debt stripe or armband only. Never apply full-body team paint to Ghost operators, suits,
  vehicles or drones.
- **Motion:** upright and economical; quiet walking raises no theatrical dust. Sprinting visibly tears camo. Weapon
  discharge creates a brief, readable camo flicker.

## Exo silhouette grammar

The shared Ghost body is upright and humanoid, with oversized shoulders, a truncated no-face visor and hardpoints
that read as sealed ports until used.

- **Base Ghost:** matte plate, seamless joints and a narrow visor slit.
- **Judge (ID 56):** thinner arms, antenna crown and an illuminated key-bracer for backdoor work; visually fragile,
  precise and electronic.
- **Warden (ID 57):** heavy greaves and folded shield petals on the back; petals open into a defensive corridor.
- **Shade (ID 58):** elongated limbs, cloak panels and minimal weapon bulk; the clearest Reaper/Mole spotter read.
- **Sergeant / Pack hierarchy (ID 59):** short command mast and twin shoulder mesh nodes linking three juniors.
- **Mk I→III (ID 63):** Mk I is riveted and scavenged; Mk II is factory-smooth and integrated; Mk III carries dual
  hardpoint “angel” wings and visible Conductor lace. Each band must be distinguishable without UI text.
- **Named aces (ID 64):** unique heraldry scar and patron-gold debt numeral, not a whole-body palette swap. G10 art
  supports three early aces, six additional progressive aces and the final-unlock presentation; G10 skirmish uses
  era gates, while G15 owns campaign-chapter placement.
- **Hardened joints / redundant battery (IDs 52, 53):** joint collars visibly resist nets and foam; the reserve cell
  is identifiable, and a battery swap produces a brief intentional shimmer tell.
- **Execution stance (ID 60):** a deliberate close HVT/officer posture, never a generic attack animation; it must
  communicate the intel-for-heat decision without gore.

## Vehicles

- **Silent Rotor / gunship flip (ID 13):** beetle-smooth insertion body with no visible prop disc. The one-way combat
  reveal opens panels into ugly gun blisters: stealth beauty becomes extraction brute force.
- **Mole multipath (ID 14):** mud-caked drill-snout coffin, never polished; alternate exit/collapse hardware reads
  as tunnel machinery rather than Machine burrow technology.
- **Ghost Runner tether (ID 15):** low wedge, no windshield and exo-shaped seats; remote-driving state needs a clear
  but restrained signal tell.
- **Stealth APC mobile vault (ID 16 is not selected):** do not require the mobile-vault feature. The roster Stealth
  APC retains the approved **civilian truck lie**: ordinary shell closed, ribbed armour and Ghost hatch exposed.
- **Guardian Mule (IDs 17, 44):** spider-legged supply crate. Guardian posture spreads the legs; last-denial
  overcharge exposes a panic-gold core tell before it destroys suit cores to prevent capture.

## Drones and counters

- **Door-kick micro-drone (ID 9):** compact breach/mark device that visibly goes first through a door or hatch.
- **Wiretap drone (ID 10):** perches at relays and reads as a listening tool, not a combat turret.
- **Spider drone (IDs 32, 37):** palm-sized, too many articulated knees, able to crawl walls/ceilings, descend into
  bunkers and hatches, plant limpets and cut power. Climbing must be readable at gameplay scale.
- **Reaper (IDs 32, 38):** rotorless flying knife/manta with one underside eye; silent high loiter and designation,
  but a loud, obvious death when discovered.
- **Reaper magazine (ID 40):** pad-only swappable recon camera, EMP dart, backdoor spike and thermite seed modules;
  silhouettes/icons must distinguish the loaded mode.
- **Nest rack (ID 41):** a honeycomb wall of sleeping spiders with occupied/empty/damaged states; burning it reads as
  a meaningful priority raid.
- **Counter spider-killers (IDs 32, 49):** bright, stubby “police” foam/flechette/ultrasonic bots contrast sharply
  with Ghost darkness. Sticky foam and wall-clearing action must be obvious, fair counters.

## Buildings

- **Ghost wing / vault core (ID 24):** underground volume; the normal surface read is hatch and vent only.
- **Adaptive kill-house (ID 25):** shifting interior light and cutaway geometry make the changed training layout
  legible after a failure.
- **Briefing crypt (ID 26):** chapel-brutalist room with a patron-gold debt altar for doctrine commitment.
- **Suit reliquary (ID 27):** glass coffins displaying recovered enemy parts; “steal → become” without Machine glow.
- **Exo Armory:** underground outfit floor under Ghost campus; sealed bay doors, suit racks, bay occupancy lights and
  brief battery-swap shimmer. Stage locker → full → black Armory scale reads in bay count. See `G10_EXO_ARMORY.md`.
- **Black market contrast:** cells trade beneath neon and tarps; Ghost sites stay dark, cold and controlled.

## Art acceptance

- Four facings preserve each role read; icons remain identifiable without colour alone.
- Hidden/revealed, camo-tear, magazine, nest occupancy and vehicle-transform states have distinct assets.
- Gold remains a debt marker, not ornamental team fill.
- Contact sheets compare ragged cells beside the too-clean Ghost Wing and bright Counter-Ghost bots.
- Any asset that needs full-body team paint, chrome camo or text labels to identify its role fails this bible.
