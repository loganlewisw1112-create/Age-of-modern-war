# G61-OFF61: officers who fight like trained leaders (MAX_TOKENS 750000, MAX_TOOL_CALLS 140)
Read G61_COMMON.md first. Flow name: officer61.

Captains and lieutenants run companies and platoons through the doctrine (doctrine.gd, doc_operational.gd,
doc_tactical.gd) and Ops missions (ops.gd, ops_missions.gd). Used by enemy AI, ally AI, waves and the player's AI
generals (GEN61 issues orders; CHAIN61 delivers them; you execute). Make it deep and real.

## Battle drills (data-driven in doctrine_rules.json where practical)
1. **Stack up and breach:** before assaulting a building/garrison/trench, the platoon stacks at a covered point beside
   it, then assaults together (no trickling in one by one).
2. **Bounding overwatch:** one section moves while the other covers from good positions; alternating and successive
   bounds; travelling (faster, looser) when no contact is expected.
3. **React to contact:** return fire, take nearest cover/height, report up (CHAIN61), then the lieutenant decides by
   belief: assault if weak, fix-and-flank if equal, break contact (smoke if available, bound back) if strong.
4. **Fix and flank:** one element suppresses while another moves on a covered route to a flank point >=60 deg off the
   axis; assault on the flank's signal; L-shape and pincer variants.
5. **Formations per situation:** column (road/forest/march), wedge (unknown contact), line (assault/defence), echelon
   (exposed flank), file (narrow ground); wider spacing under artillery (uses SQUAD61 formation shapes).
6. **Defence in depth:** primary/alternate positions, sectors of fire, fall back by bounds, counter-attack reserve.
7. **Movement across a battlefield:** route choice avoids open ground under observed enemy fire and known minefields,
   prefers cover/height, phase lines with halts to regroup.
8. **Officer realism:** officers stay behind the line, casualties lower tempo, a platoon whose lieutenant dies is
   briefly leaderless until the senior squad leader takes over (integrate with Companies promotion and CHAIN61),
   officers report to the captain in short readable lines, ammo/casualty-aware (pull back to resupply).
9. **Traits and experience:** read REAL61's leader traits (cautious, bold, tactician, ...) and veterancy to shape choices
   (e.g. bold assaults at worse odds, tactician flanks more); neutral until REAL61 posts its API.

## API for GEN61 / CHAIN61 / PLAN61
`Doctrine.execute_order(w, fid, node, order)` (order = intent dict from CommandNet: type take/hold/raid/screen/
breach/withdraw, target, phase lines, deadline, main_effort) and `Doctrine.drill_state(w, fid, node)` (current drill,
phase, readable line) for UI. Post `fact doctrine.order_api` as a stub within 15 calls.
