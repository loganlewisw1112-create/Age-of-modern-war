# G61-GEN61: AI commanders as full generals (MAX_TOKENS 750000, MAX_TOOL_CALLS 140)
Read G61_COMMON.md first. Flow name: gen61.

Today a player AI Commander slot (ai_command.gd + commanders.gd) needs the player to pick objective
{take|hold|raid|escort} + cell, then drives it through Doctrine.plan_for. The owner wants **full generals**.

## 1. Autonomous operations: objective `{type: "auto"}` (the default for new slots; old types still work)
- Decides from its side's **belief** (CHAIN61 Intel API; until it lands use the side's fog-visible enemies), re-plans on a
  cadence, and picks an operation by score: **strike** (concentrated attack on a weak/valuable target), **infil/exfil**
  (spec-ops in avoiding contact, hit, out by a different route), **deliberate attack** (recon > fix > flank > assault >
  consolidate), **defend** in depth (key terrain/base when threatened), **counter-attack**, **recon in force**,
  **screen/delay**, **raid supply lines**, **regroup/refit** (pull back to resupply/heal when depleted).
- Scores use force ratio from belief, losses, ammo/fuel, terrain/height, distance, playstyle, stance and its leader
  traits (REAL61 API; treat as neutral until it lands).
- Multi-company ops when it holds an army: main effort + supporting/fixing effort + reserve, timed by phase lines.
- Mission memory: doesn't repeat a failed axis at once; tempo: pauses to consolidate after an objective; breaks off at
  the stance's loss threshold; disperses under enemy air; slots on one side coordinate (no two slots on one target).
- Issues orders **down the chain** (CHAIN61 `CommandNet.issue`; until it lands, call the current Doctrine/Ops path
  directly) as intent; OFF61 executes drills; SQUAD61 sets squad stances/formations.

## 2. War effort (full general)
- **Budget:** the player grants a slot a budget (credits/salvage/ore per minute, or a lump sum) in the Commanders panel.
  The general spends only that: trains replacement companies / reinforcement elements at the side's Company HQ
  (train_company / refill through the normal commands, so all rules apply), queues units at production buildings.
- **Fire support:** calls artillery/strikes/air the side owns (existing Strikes, Callins, Air sortie commands) on
  targets from belief; respects cooldowns and costs; never targets near its own troops (danger-close check).
- **Logistics:** orders resupply (supply trucks, bearers once BEARER61 lands, Support auto-resupply), medevac, fuel runs;
  pulls depleted companies back to refit.
- **Reports to the player:** a readable log per slot ("Op STRIKE: enemy artillery at (82,40), weak escort 3:1, 2 coys")
  and a short status (current op, phase, strength %, budget left).

## 3. UI (presentation/ui6/a/commanders_panel.gd)
"Auto (general decides)" objective as the default; budget control; current operation + phase + last 3 log lines per
slot; must pass UI fit.

## Rules
Never order a unit the player touched within 20 s; Recall returns control at once; generals use only commands a player
could issue (validated by the normal command path), so they can't cheat; enemy AI generals may reuse the same engine
(ai_controller hooks belong to OFF61's ai_army / doctrine path: coordinate by change_request).
