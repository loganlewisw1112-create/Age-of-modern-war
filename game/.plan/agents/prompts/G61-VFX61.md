# G61-VFX61: visual effects overhaul (MAX_TOKENS 750000, MAX_TOOL_CALLS 140)
Read G61_COMMON.md first. Flow name: vfx61.

Owner (2026-10-26): visual effects overhaul to match the animation overhaul, in G6.1. Today presentation/view/fx_layer.gd draws
tracers, muzzle flashes, explosions, smoke, artillery arcs, flamer jets, missile trails (cosmetic RNG, culled, zoom LOD).

Build (presentation only; never touches the sim or its hash):
1. **Weapons:** muzzle flash per weapon class (rifle, MG, cannon, rocket, artillery, laser/energy for Machine) with a light
   flash on nearby ground, tracers with glow and per-class colour/speed, shell casings for MGs, barrel smoke after cannon fire,
   backblast for rockets/AT. Muzzle positions from ANIMB61 (turret frames) via its fact.
2. **Impacts:** dirt/dust kicks on ground hits, sparks on armour, ricochets, water splashes, building debris chunks, craters and
   scorch decals that persist (capped, oldest fade).
3. **Explosions:** layered (flash, fireball, shockwave ring, debris, lingering smoke column), sized by splash; vehicle
   destruction with secondary explosions and a burning wreck (fire + smoke plume that drifts).
4. **Ambient battlefield:** drifting smoke and dust, fires spreading on burning buildings/forest, dust trails behind moving
   vehicles (by terrain type), heat haze over fires, flares/illumination over night ops if any, smoke screens (OFF61 drills tag
   "smoke"; draw a smoke cloud when that decision fires).
5. **Lighting:** soft additive light for flashes/fires/explosions over the terrain (CanvasItem light or additive sprites, cheap),
   optional subtle vignette in battle.
6. **Readability:** effects never hide selection rings or health bars; a quality setting (Low/Medium/High) in Settings
   (presentation/menu/settings.gd: ask ORCH via change_request for the one line, or add it if the settings file has a section
   API) scales particle counts; Low must keep the G6 fps.
Art: add effect sprites through the pipeline (fx generators: tools/art/fx_g2.gd, g4_units_fx.gd, g5_*_fx.gd; new files of yours).
